# Example — AI for business

## Synopsis for organizers
Most teams have tried ChatGPT, but few have changed how they work. In this talk, [audience] will see five concrete workflows that save hours every week, try two of them live via QR codes, and leave with a one-page plan to start on Monday.

## For whom
Managers and entrepreneurs without a technical background.

## Key points
- Start with one repetitive task, not with "AI transformation".
- Give AI your context: documents, examples, tone.
- A human stays the editor: check facts and numbers.

## Versions
- 15 min: 3 workflows + 1 live demo
- 45 min: 5 workflows + 2 live tasks + Q&A
- 90 min / workshop: + hands-on practice with a workbook
