# One prompt, five video generators

> Same prompt. Look what five different AI models made of it.

## Essence
Run one short video prompt (e.g. "a majestic wolf in the snow") through several generators and play the results side by side. The audience votes for the best one. It works as interaction and as an honest comparison.

## Leads to
"There is no single best AI. There is the best one for your task."
